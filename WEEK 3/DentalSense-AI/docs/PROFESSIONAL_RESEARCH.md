# Professional Industry Research

To ensure DentalSense AI mirrors actual enterprise patient feedback systems, three professional sentiment and healthcare analytics tools were analyzed:

## 1. Qualtrics - Patient Experience
- **URL**: [https://www.qualtrics.com/healthcare/patient-experience/](https://www.qualtrics.com/healthcare/patient-experience/)
- **Functionality**: Collects omnichannel feedback and uses AI (Text iQ) to run sentiment analysis and topic modeling.
- **Professional Features**: Deep dive filtering by facility, provider, and sentiment. Alert systems for highly negative reviews.
- **Lesson Learned**: Static numbers aren't enough; the ability to filter negative reviews by specific operational themes (like "Wait Time") drives actual clinical improvements.
- **Application in DentalSense**: Implemented the "Negative Feedback by Theme" metric and the Feedback Explorer allowing combinatorial filtering of Sentiment + Theme.

## 2. RepuGen
- **URL**: [https://www.repugen.com/](https://www.repugen.com/)
- **Functionality**: Healthcare reputation management and patient sentiment tracker.
- **Professional Features**: KPI dashboard for retention, positive review ratios, and direct competitor benchmarking.
- **Lesson Learned**: Healthcare administrators need immediate visual confirmation of their overall health score upon login.
- **Application in DentalSense**: Designed the 4 top-level KPI cards (Total Reviews, Positive Rate, Negative Rate, Avg Confidence) directly at the top of the dashboard for instant read-out.

## 3. MonkeyLearn - Sentiment Analysis
- **URL**: [https://monkeylearn.com/sentiment-analysis/](https://monkeylearn.com/sentiment-analysis/)
- **Functionality**: General SaaS for text analysis, sentiment, and intent.
- **Professional Features**: Drag-and-drop CSV batch testing and clean probability (confidence) scores.
- **Lesson Learned**: Users need to see exactly *why* a model made a decision, which means exposing the statistical confidence.
- **Application in DentalSense**: Included the raw `confidence` score generated by the Hugging Face model in the UI, rather than hiding it behind the Sentiment label.

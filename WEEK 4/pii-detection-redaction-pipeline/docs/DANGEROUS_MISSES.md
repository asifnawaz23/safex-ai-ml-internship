# Dangerous Misses

Machine-readable sources:

- `output/metrics/dangerous_misses.csv` and `output/metrics/dangerous_misses.json` (generated by `python -m pii_pipeline.cli evaluate`)
- the "before fixes" selection in `output/metrics/initial/dangerous_misses_initial.json`

This file restates those rows. If they ever disagree, the CSV is authoritative.

## How the cases were chosen

- **Candidate pool.** The cases come from the **first full improved run** (before any fix), which had 456 false negatives. A false negative here means a planted occurrence that was not matched exactly: it was missed, partial or given the wrong label.
- **Ordering.**
  - Cases are ordered by entity severity, following the brief: CNIC > PHONE_NUMBER > EMAIL_ADDRESS > PERSON > ADDRESS > POSTAL_CODE > CUSTOMER_ID.
  - Within that, missed comes before partial, which comes before wrong label.
  - Ties are broken by annotation id.
  - At most 2 cases are taken per diagnosis, so that the 15 cases cover distinct failure modes.
- **Result.** **15 actual misses, 0 constructed cases.** There were more than 15 actual misses, so no adversarial cases had to be invented.
- **Verification status.** The status is computed by re-checking each annotation id against the **current** improved predictions:
  - `DETECTED_AFTER_FIX`: the occurrence is now an exact match, and the named regression test passes.
  - `NOT_FIXED_BY_DESIGN`: the occurrence is still missed. A test documents the miss, so a behaviour change would fail the suite.
- **Excerpts.** These are synthetic, and each PII value is masked to its first and last two characters (`privacy.mask_value`).

**Summary:** 10 cases are `DETECTED_AFTER_FIX` (DM-001, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 013, 014, 015) and 5 are `NOT_FIXED_BY_DESIGN` (DM-002, 009, 010, 011, 012).

Address and landmark misses are not among the 15. The ordering puts PERSON ahead of ADDRESS, and the per-diagnosis cap filled the list first. Those misses are still real: in the final run all 43 landmark-style addresses are missed, with exact recall 0.0000. They are reported in `docs/EVALUATION_REPORT.md` (Section 7) and in `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`.

## Why each entity type is sensitive

These statements are repeated in the `why_sensitive` column:

- **CNIC:** a national identity number. It is a lifelong unique identifier that can be used for identity fraud and for linking records across systems.
- **Phone:** a personal mobile number. It is a direct contact channel and enables harassment, SIM-swap and social-engineering attacks.
- **Email:** a personal email address. It is a direct contact channel and a common login identifier.
- **Person:** the name of a customer or staff member. It is a direct identifier, especially when combined with an address or phone.

## Case details

### DM-001: CNIC, label glued to the number (REC-00546, internal_operational_note)

- **Excerpt:** `...r Account - UB*****35 / Customer NIC:42*********43 / Action: Verified customer identity...`
- **Why detection failed / root cause:** In `NIC:<13 digits>`, spaCy treats the label and number as one token. Presidio's context enhancer therefore finds no context word before the number. The plain 13-digit pattern stays at 0.30, below the 0.35 threshold.
- **Risk:** The full CNIC is left in the redacted output.
- **Fix:** A label-anchored plain CNIC pattern, with a lookbehind on CNIC / NIC / National ID / NADRA ID / Identity No. and a score of 0.70.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_cnic_label_glued_to_number`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX

### DM-002: CNIC, plain 13 digits without context (CHAL-013, internal_operational_note)

- **Excerpt:** `Customer read out 41*********93 twice on the call.`
- **Why detection failed / root cause:** No context word appears in the 5 words before the number. The plain pattern scores 0.30 on purpose, so that 13-digit barcodes are not redacted as CNICs.
- **Risk:** An unlabelled CNIC survives redaction.
- **Proposed fix:** Not fixed. Lowering the score would turn barcodes into CNIC detections. In the final run, 0 of 22 barcode traps were hit. Mitigation: human review of documents with long digit runs. A reviewer-facing flag for unlabelled 13-digit numbers is future work.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_cnic_plain_no_context_documented_tradeoff`. It asserts the current trade-off.
- **Status:** NOT_FIXED_BY_DESIGN. In the final run, 8 CNICs are still missed, all of the plain kind (edge-case tag `cnic_plain`: 54 of 62 detected).

### DM-003: CNIC, space-separated (CHAL-017, contact_form)

- **Excerpt:** `CNIC: 21*********** 7 (customer typed it with spaces).`
- **Why detection failed / root cause:** `XXXXX XXXXXXX X` is a held-out format. The recogniser only knew hyphenated and contiguous CNICs.
- **Risk:** The full CNIC is left in the redacted output.
- **Fix:** A space-separated 5-7-1 pattern with a score of 0.80.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_cnic_space_separated`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX. The `cnic_spaced` tag is now 10 of 10.

### DM-004: Phone, dot-separated (REC-00145, delivery_order)

- **Excerpt:** `...Ho********************************************la. Please call 03********68 on arrival.`
- **Why detection failed / root cause:** `03XX.XXXXXXX` is a held-out format. The custom separator class was `[ -]` only, and the built-in `PhoneRecognizer` (no PK region) did not report the number in these contexts.
- **Risk:** The mobile number is left in the redacted output.
- **Fix:** Allow `.` as the local separator.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_phone_dot_separated`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX. The `phone_dot_separated` tag went from 0.8276 in the baseline to 1.0000 in the improved run.

### DM-005: Phone, 4-3-4 grouping (REC-00134, contact_form)

- **Excerpt:** `...il=na********************rg; phone=03*********07; topic=delivery`
- **Why detection failed / root cause:** The held-out grouping `03XX XXX XXXX` had no custom pattern, and the built-in recogniser missed it.
- **Risk:** The mobile number is left in the redacted output.
- **Fix:** A 4-3-4 local pattern with a score of 0.80.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_phone_4_3_4`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX. The `phone_local_4_3_4` tag is now 20 of 20.

### DM-006: Phone, partial span (CHAL-019, delivery_note)

- **Excerpt:** `Contact:Ra*******ed(03********61)`
- **Observed before the fix:** The `PhoneRecognizer` predicted [19:32], which includes the opening bracket, while the ground truth is [20:32].
- **Root cause:** Two PHONE_NUMBER detections were produced: the built-in one with the bracket and the exact custom one. The old normalisation rule "larger span wins" kept the built-in span.
- **Risk:** Low for redaction, because the number is still covered. Counted as an exact-span miss.
- **Fix:** Same-label containment precedence: a custom recogniser's span wins over a built-in one.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_phone_custom_span_precedence`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX

### DM-007: Email, `key=` swallowed (REC-00002, contact_form)

- **Excerpt:** `...ntact; name=Pa***********in; email=p.*****************om; phone=03*******77; topic=new conn...`
- **Observed before the fix:** The `EmailRecognizer` predicted [43:70], while the ground truth is [49:70].
- **Root cause:** Presidio's email regex accepts `=` in the local part. In key=value text, `email=<address>` was therefore matched as one address.
- **Risk:** Low for redaction, since the key is over-redacted, but the span is wrong and downstream parsers lose the key.
- **Fix:** Span hygiene that drops a leading `key=` from EMAIL_ADDRESS spans. It is applied in **both** modes.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_email_field_key_prefix_trimmed`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX. Baseline and improved EMAIL_ADDRESS recall are both 1.0000 now; both were 0.8886 initially.

### DM-008: Email, upper case (REC-00086, contact_form)

- **Excerpt:** `...b-contact; name=Ta*******za; email=TA******************OM; cnic=35***********-1; topic=billi...`
- **Observed before the fix:** The `EmailRecognizer` predicted [39:67], while the ground truth is [45:67].
- **Root cause:** Same as DM-007. The upper case was **not** the cause; the `uppercase_email` tag is 22 of 22 in both modes.
- **Fix, test and status:** The same fix and test as DM-007. DETECTED_AFTER_FIX.

### DM-009: Person, all lower case (CHAL-015, internal_operational_note)

- **Excerpt:** `spoke to sh*********si about the leaking dispenser, will ...`
- **Root cause:** An all-lower-case name in free text. `LabelledPersonRecognizer` is case-sensitive by design, and `en_core_web_sm` does not tag lower-case names.
- **Risk:** The customer name is left in the redacted output.
- **Proposed fix:** Not fixed. A name gazetteer would also match ordinary words, and it would be fitted to the generator's name list. Human review is required.
- **Test:** `test_dm_person_lowercase_name_expected_miss` (strict xfail).
- **Status:** NOT_FIXED_BY_DESIGN. The `lowercase_name` tag is 0.1395 in both modes.

### DM-010: Person, unlabelled in prose (REC-00006, internal_operational_note)

- **Excerpt:** `Visited No********al today about account UB*****73. Cus...`
- **Root cause:** `en_core_web_sm` does not tag the name, and no label precedes it for the pattern recogniser. The small model is the only NER signal for unlabelled names. Larger spaCy and Transformer models were excluded by the 8 GB RAM and disk constraints.
- **Risk:** The customer name is left in the redacted output.
- **Proposed fix:** Not fixed in this prototype. It needs a stronger NER model or more context patterns.
- **Test:** `test_dm_person_unlabelled_in_prose_expected_miss` (strict xfail).
- **Status:** NOT_FIXED_BY_DESIGN

### DM-011: Person, three-token signature (REC-00048, internal_operational_note)

- **Excerpt:** `... Will call back on 03********61. - Al************ar`
- **Root cause:** A staff signature after `- ` at the end of a note. `en_core_web_sm` does not tag it, and no label precedes it.
- **Risk:** The staff name is left in the redacted output.
- **Proposed fix:** Not fixed. A signature pattern (`- Name Name`) is possible but risks FPs on capitalised phrases, so stronger NER is the preferred route.
- **Test:** `test_dm_person_three_token_signature_expected_miss` (strict xfail).
- **Status:** NOT_FIXED_BY_DESIGN. The `three_token_name` tag improved overall from 0.5263 to 0.6947, but this case is still missed.

### DM-012: Person, ALL CAPS (REC-00028, delivery_note)

- **Excerpt:** `DN-751194 | drv: OM*******SI | to: Us*******al | +9************01...`
- **Root cause:** An ALL-CAPS name after a short label (`drv:`). `en_core_web_sm` does not tag it, and the labelled-person pattern requires capitalised tokens.
- **Risk:** The driver name is left in the redacted output.
- **Proposed fix:** Not fixed. An upper-case variant would match headings such as `DELIVERY NOTE`. Human review is required.
- **Test:** `test_dm_person_uppercase_name_expected_miss` (strict xfail).
- **Status:** NOT_FIXED_BY_DESIGN. The `uppercase_name` tag is 0.1087 in both modes.

### DM-013: Person, span runs across `|` (REC-00127, delivery_order)

- **Excerpt:** `order pls - name ha**********hi | ph +9***********11 | addr Sh**************************an...`
- **Observed before the fix:** The `SpacyRecognizer` predicted [17:40], while the ground truth is [17:31].
- **Root cause:** The spaCy PERSON span ran across the `|` field delimiter into the phone field.
- **Risk:** Over-redaction of the neighbouring field, and an exact-span miss.
- **Fix:** Span hygiene that ends PERSON spans at field delimiters, line breaks and digits.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_person_span_cut_at_field_delimiter`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX

### DM-014: Person, no whitespace before the phone (CHAL-019, delivery_note)

- **Excerpt:** `Contact:Ra*******ed(03********61)`
- **Observed before the fix:** The `SpacyRecognizer` predicted [8:32], while the ground truth is [8:19].
- **Root cause:** The PERSON span continued through `(` into the phone number.
- **Risk:** Over-redaction and a wrong label on the phone digits.
- **Fix, test and status:** The same fix and test as DM-013. DETECTED_AFTER_FIX.

### DM-015: Person, label included (CHAL-008, subscription_record)

- **Excerpt:** `Subscriber Ma***********ri at Fl*************************************00...`
- **Observed before the fix:** The `SpacyRecognizer` predicted [0:26], while the ground truth is [11:26].
- **Root cause:** spaCy included the form label `Subscriber` in the PERSON span.
- **Risk:** The label word is over-redacted, and the span is an exact-span miss.
- **Fix:** Span hygiene that strips leading form labels and titles from PERSON spans.
- **Regression test:** `test_dm_person_leading_label_trimmed`
- **Status:** DETECTED_AFTER_FIX
